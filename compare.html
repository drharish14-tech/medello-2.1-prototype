ChatGPT removed (vs Daylight AI)

  • Center AI orb / sparkle in the tab bar (opened Tend chat).
  • AI sparkle buttons beside each med row on Meds (Ask AI).
  • Hold to take press-and-hold CTA on the next-dose card.
  • Tell Tend AI + “Snap — AI reads…” paths on Add.
  • Ask about your medicines… chat sheet entry from the orb.
  • Floating coral + FAB over Today (Daylight pattern).

ChatGPT added

  • Family as a labelled 5th tab (replaces the center orb slot).
  • Your people. patient Family screen (Alex card, check-in, quiet hours).
  • Dose actions as explicit Log (Taken / Snooze / Skip) instead of Hold-to-take + Ask AI.
  • Onboarding welcome + role frames (patient vs caregiver) — new chrome.
  • Copy / layout refresh: “Your day.”, timeline card, unconfirmed Review banner.
2.1 App Store direction (facts → recommendation): Family chrome is in the ChatGPT Patient & Family pack and matches the interactive prototype’s 5-tab nav. Full center AI orb + Tell AI / Ask AI CTAs are Daylight-era and are not in the ChatGPT pack. Per PR #38 direction: keep Family chrome for 2.1; do not ship the full AI orb as primary chrome.
